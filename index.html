Synth Ballet // Synthesized Movement Lab

Research toward a collaborative system for dancers and electronic musicians to perform live together. Designed as a series of prompts and rules with dancer Kaylin Maggard and musician Kengchakaj Kengkarna, a prototype was filmed in February 2023 by Danny Garfield.





A longer iteration, adding dancer Colin Heininger, was performed at James Cohan Gallery's Art in Conversation series in May 2023.



One of the prompts was published in Games for a Rainy Day by TXTbooks for the 2024 New York Art Book Fair.

Read To Me prototyped a space that visually reacted to the live reading of poetry and prose, created as a Resident Artist at CultureHub in 2018. It was later exhibited at the 2019 Mission Creek Festival at the University of Iowa and the 2019 NextNOW Fest at The Clarice Performing Arts Center at the University of Maryland.

Deirdre Coyle wrote about her experience as a reader for Electric Literature. The Google Cloud Blog covered the project and it was featured on Google's Instagram.

Oren Shoham and Sam Von Ehren worked on the back end. Flan Falacci, Milan Koerner-Safrata, and Dennis Carr developed new graphical interpretations based on the initial prototype.

At my friend Maya's birthday/goodbye-going-to-grad-school party in 2021, she used a QR code linked to an open shared Spotify playlist that everyone could contribute to in lieu of a DJ. It was wonderful, and I'd never put together how easy it was to do something like that until then. A few weeks later, frustrated with how bad the algorithmic- and revenue-driven playlist culture is on Spotify, I decided to create a QR code linked to a playlist on a new Spotify account, and posted four signs on the street inviting anyone to contribute to it.



It now has almost 13 hours of music, all provided by random passers-by. I wouldn't really call this project a success, or really even a project, but an attempt. I don't really like Spotify. It treats artists terribly, and worse, doesn't see that as a problem. I spent a lot of time wondering if I could interact with the platform differently, use it in a way that might feel good. I did not succeed, and stopped using the service not long after. But here's a link to the playlist—
